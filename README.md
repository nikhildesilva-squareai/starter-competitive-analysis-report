## Overview

Analyse 5 AI-powered products in a specific market vertical (e.g., AI writing tools, AI customer service, AI recruiting). Create a comprehensive report that identifies market gaps, compares features, evaluates pricing strategies, and recommends a competitive positioning strategy.

This project is part of the **AI Product Management** course, where you'll learn to lead AI product teams, define strategy, and bring AI-powered products to market.

## What You'll Learn

- Evaluate AI products across multiple dimensions (features, UX, pricing, AI quality)
- Identify market whitespace and unmet user needs
- Create comparison frameworks and scoring rubrics
- Develop actionable strategic recommendations

## Tech Stack

`Research` `Analysis` `Presentation`

## Approach

Pick a specific AI market segment. Sign up for free trials of 5 competitors. Use each product for at least 30 minutes. Document strengths, weaknesses, pricing, and AI capabilities. Synthesise findings into a strategic framework.

## Deliverables

- Feature comparison matrix with scoring
- SWOT analysis for each competitor
- Market positioning map (2x2 matrix)
- Pricing analysis with tier comparison
- Strategic recommendations (2-3 pages)

## Tips & Guidance

- Actually USE the products — screenshots of real interactions are gold
- Test edge cases: how does each product handle unusual inputs?
- Look at review sites (G2, Capterra) for real user sentiment
- Include publicly available metrics (funding, headcount, growth) where possible
